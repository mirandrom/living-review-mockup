Living reviews of climate-related disclosure policy

Proof of concept. Three self-contained living reviews run by AI agents following written protocols, starting from the IFRS Fellows concept note (Bolton et al., 2025). Release 2 (24 Nov 2026) recodes the pilot under amended protocols: mechanisms marked tested or proposed, records linked to policies, places and years, and a history for every finding. Records are agent-coded and not yet human-checked.
